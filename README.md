# Mindstream

**A quiet place to think.** Private journaling with a bring‑your‑own‑key AI companion.

- ✍️ **Write** with a daily prompt (AI‑tailored to what you've been writing about), a 5‑point mood picker and tags.
- ✨ **Reflect** — a 2‑sentence summary, themes, a gentle follow‑up question, a kinder reframe, and one tiny step for tomorrow.
- 📈 **Timeline** — every entry, plus a 14‑day mood trend and your streak.
- 🔎 **Weekly insight** — patterns, wins, one thing to be gentle about, and an experiment for next week.
- 🎚️ Companion tone: warm, direct or playful.

BYOK: Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI‑compatible endpoint. Entries never leave your phone unless you tap Reflect. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Jetpack Compose · Material 3 Expressive · Room · AGP 9.4. `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease`
